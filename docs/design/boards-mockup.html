Boards mockup: thin custom UI

A clickable sketch of the Ideas, Tasks, Projects and Decisions boards, styled after the UI design brief (docs/design/ui-brief.md): server-rendered columns of status, with a small script for dragging and moving. The data is sample data. Saving, syncing and conflicts are simulated.

Drag a card by its ⠿ grip, or use Move to…. Move a ready idea to Transplanted, an active project to Completed or Archived, or a decision to ACCEPTED or SUPERSEDED, to see the approval paths. On Tasks, look for the red blocked by and green unblocked chips. Switch to iPhone for the 375 px layout, and use the moon or sun in the app's top bar to preview the other appearance.

Ideas

What the mockup shows

Columns are statuses

Each column is one status from spec §7, in lifecycle order. Nothing else decides where a card sits, so a card moved on the Mac lands in the right column after the next fetch. Cards sort by updated, newest first.

Terminal columns (Transplanted, Composted, Cancelled, Archived, SUPERSEDED, EXPIRED) start as folded strips. Click one to open it.

Only allowed moves

While you drag, forbidden columns dim and refuse the drop; columns that need approval get a violet outline. Move to… lists only allowed targets, and names the likely next step where there is one, such as Accept… or Supersede….

Drag by the grip

Cards drag only by the ⠿ grip, which has touch-action: none. The rest of the card scrolls the board normally on a phone, and tapping the title opens Record detail.

Move to… works everywhere

A native <select> on every card. On iPhone it opens the system picker. It is also the keyboard and screen-reader path. Without JavaScript the boards can be read but not changed.

Sync state

Every move shows Saving…, then the committed SHA in green, or pending in amber when GitHub can't be reached. The top bar sums it up for the page: Up to date · 3e9b12f.

409 conflict

If the file changed since the page was rendered, the server rejects the move. The dialog shows base, current and yours for the fields that differ, and offers to move anyway on the current version or keep it (§8.4).

Approval paths

Ready → Transplanted starts the promotion wizard (§10). A project from Active to Completed or Archived, and a decision into ACCEPTED, SUPERSEDED or EXPIRED, open the confirmation with the Markdown diff and a passkey check (§9.2).

Blocked, two ways

The Blocked column is the status, set by hand. A red blocked by chip shows open blocks links in any column, and moving such a task to In progress or Done asks first.

Moving a blocker to Done also moves the Blocked tasks it was the last open blocker of to Ready, in the same commit: “Done (+1 unblocked)”. Try OS-T-2B7KC. After a cancelled blocker, a green unblocked chip offers the move.

Decisions board

Columns are the upper-case ADR states. A move rewrites both the frontmatter status and the body's * Status: line. SUPERSEDED needs a newer decision that supersedes this one; without it the drop offers to draft one.

Phone

The app shell stays; the sidebar collapses to icons, the breadcrumb and sync label shorten, and the board scrolls sideways one column at a time with status chips to jump. Tap targets stay large.

Appearance

The page follows the system light or dark setting. The moon or sun in the top bar overrides it for this browser; in the product that choice lives in localStorage and is applied from /static, since the CSP allows no inline script.

Changes from the Mac

The server fetches every 60 s. The page asks whether the board changed when it becomes visible and every 60 s, and swaps the board in place if no move is in flight.
